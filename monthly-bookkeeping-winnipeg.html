Monthly Bookkeeping FAQ

How much does monthly bookkeeping cost in Winnipeg?

Our Bookkeeping plan is $299/month for companies with less than 100 monthly transactions. Bookkeeping & Tax is $499/month and adds corporate and personal tax filing. On a free call, Jennifer tells you which plan fits.

Who actually does my books?

One dedicated, in-house bookkeeper. All our bookkeepers are located in Manitoba, near Winnipeg. You talk to the same person each time.

How fast does my bookkeeper reply?

Every plan includes a 24-hour response guarantee. The Bookkeeping & Tax plan adds priority support.

What does a monthly bookkeeper do?

Your bookkeeper categorizes every transaction, reconciles your bank accounts monthly, tracks payroll and expenses, files GST/PST and T4/T5, and delivers a balance sheet and income statement. You stay audit-ready.

Can I switch from my current bookkeeper or QuickBooks?

Yes. We migrate your books from QuickBooks or any other software and keep continuity. We use our own secure systems, so you need no extra software. If you ever leave, you get your full books back in Excel and PDF.

Will you find deductions I am missing?

We actively search for missed deductions, like business expenses, home office costs and travel. On the call, Jennifer tells you where your books likely fall short.

I am behind on my books. Can you still help?

Yes. We recover missing documents, file or amend overdue taxes and handle CRA communication. Then monthly bookkeeping keeps you current.
