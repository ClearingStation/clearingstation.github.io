Catch-up bookkeeping questions

What is catch up bookkeeping?

Catch-up bookkeeping means bringing books that are months or years behind up to date. We record past transactions, reconcile bank and credit card accounts, and prepare what you need for overdue GST and tax filings.

How much does catch up bookkeeping cost?

It depends on how far behind you are and how many transactions you have. We give you a fixed price on a free call, so you know the full cost before we start. To stay current afterwards, monthly plans start from $299/month.

I'm years behind on bookkeeping. Is it too late?

No. We have helped businesses that were years behind get current and stay that way. We assess your situation, recover missing documents and file or amend overdue taxes.

Do I need to organize my receipts before I call?

No. Call as you are. On the call we tell you which documents actually matter, and we help you recover the ones that are missing.

Will I get CRA penalties for late filing?

Late filings can bring penalties and interest. We file overdue returns with the aim to minimize penalties, and we handle CRA communication for you. On the call we explain what applies to your case.

What is the difference between bookkeeping cleanup and catch-up?

Catch-up fills in months that were never recorded. Bookkeeping cleanup fixes books that exist but are wrong or messy. Many businesses need some of both, and we do both.

Do you work with businesses outside Winnipeg?

Yes. Our bookkeepers are in-house and based in Manitoba near Winnipeg, and we work with businesses across Manitoba.
