Common questions

Self-Employed Tax in Winnipeg: FAQ

When is the self-employed tax deadline in Manitoba?

If you or your spouse are self-employed, your personal return is due June 15. Any balance owing is still due April 30. Interest starts on unpaid tax after April 30.

What is a T2125?

The T2125 is the CRA form for business or professional income. Sole proprietors and freelancers file it with their personal return. It shows your income and your business expenses.

Do I need to register for GST as a freelancer?

Small suppliers do not have to register until their sales pass the CRA limit. Some freelancers register early by choice. On the call, Jennifer tells you whether you need to register yet.

What can I deduct as a self-employed person?

Common business costs include a part of your home office, phone, internet, software, equipment and the business use of your vehicle. Good records matter. We look for the costs you can claim and keep them CRA-ready.

Why did the CRA send me an instalment reminder?

When no tax is withheld from your income, the CRA can ask you to pay tax in instalments during the year. We check your situation and tell you what to pay.

Do I need a bookkeeper or just a freelancer accountant at tax time?

Many small freelancers only need a yearly return: $450 to file, or $900 if we also do your year of books. When your volume grows, our Bookkeeping plan is $299/month. We tell you honestly which one fits.

Should I incorporate to save tax?

It depends on how much you earn and how much you leave in the business. For many freelancers it does not pay off yet. We look at your numbers and give you a straight answer.
