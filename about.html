Jennifer Flaming

Founder, ClearingStation Ltd.

Jennifer has spent more than eleven years keeping books and filing taxes for Manitoba businesses. She has handled more than 100 CRA audits for clients, from the first letter to the final answer. She is a CRA authorized representative, so she speaks to the CRA for you.

Her clients include physicians, clinics, lawyers, contractors, landlords and tech companies. What they share: they want one person who knows their file and picks up the phone.

Every client still talks to Jennifer on the first call. She tells you plainly what your books need, what it costs, and when the cheaper plan is enough.
