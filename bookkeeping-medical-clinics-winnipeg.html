Sound Familiar?

Clinic owners and office managers tell us the same things — medical, dental, physio, chiro, massage and optometry alike.

"Money comes in from five places."

Provincial billing, insurers, direct billing and patient payments at the front desk. Matching deposits to visits takes days.

"I hope the splits are right."

Each associate has a different split or overhead arrangement. You do the math in a spreadsheet and hope nobody asks.

"Is she a contractor or an employee?"

Some practitioners invoice you, others are on payroll with the front desk and assistants. It is easy to get wrong.

"GST? On some things, I think."

Most health services are exempt. Products and some services are not. You are not sure where your line is.

"Month-end is chaos."

Rent, equipment, supplies, payouts and payroll all land at once. The books are always weeks behind.

"Which part of the clinic makes money?"

You know total revenue. You do not know which practitioner or service line actually pays the lease.
