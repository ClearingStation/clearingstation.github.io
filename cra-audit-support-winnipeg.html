Sample letter

How to Read a CRA Letter

Look for these four parts on your letter. The notes say what each part means and what we do.

Sample. Written by ClearingStation to explain a review letter. Not a CRA document.

Date: [date of the letter]

Reference number: [upper right corner]

[Your name and address]

1 · Reference number CRA puts a reference number in the upper right corner and asks for it on your reply. Keep the letter in your hand when Jennifer calls.

Re: Review of your income tax return for [tax year]

We are reviewing the following claim on your return: [claim].

2 · What it is This line names the tax year and the type of letter: a review, a request for information or an audit. CRA says a review is not a tax audit. We tell you which one you have and how serious it is.

Please send us:

  • [receipts or invoices for the claim]
  • [bank statements for the period]

You or your authorized representative can send the documents.

3 · What CRA asks for Often it is less than you fear. We tell you what to gather first and what can wait. Once you authorize us as your representative, we gather and organize the documents and prepare the response.

Reply by: [date]

If you need more time, call the telephone number at the bottom of this letter.

4 · Reply date This is your deadline. We look at it and tell you if asking for more time makes sense in your case, and how.

Common questions

CRA Audit Support: Common Questions

Is a CRA review letter the same as an audit?

Not always. CRA sends reviews, requests for information and full audits. A review is often smaller, but all of them need a correct answer on time. On the call we tell you which one you have.

Should I call CRA myself first?

We suggest you talk to us first. What you say to CRA becomes part of your file. A 10-minute call with us helps you avoid answers you later need to correct.

Can you talk to CRA for me?

Yes. Once you authorize us as your representative, we handle government communication for you. Representation and advocacy is part of our CRA audit support.

My deadline is very close. Is it too late?

Call us now, even if the deadline is today. We look at your letter and tell you whether more time is possible and what to send first.

My books are behind or receipts are missing. Can you still help?

Yes. We help businesses years behind get current. We recover missing documents and file or amend overdue returns as part of the audit response.

How much does CRA audit help cost in Winnipeg?

It depends on the letter, the years involved and the state of your records. The first call is free and you get a quote on that call, with no obligation.

What happens after the audit is done?

We help with post-audit compliance, so your books and filings stay in order. If you want, you can then move to a monthly bookkeeping plan with a dedicated bookkeeper.
