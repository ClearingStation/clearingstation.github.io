Sound Familiar?

If you sell real estate or own rentals, one of these may sound like you.

"Three deals closed in May. Then nothing until August."

Uneven commission income makes CRA instalments and tax set-aside a guess every quarter.

"I spend a fortune on marketing and my car."

Signs, listings, staging, client gifts, phone, kilometres. You are not sure what CRA accepts or how to prove it.

You collect GST on commissions, but filing it, and claiming GST back on expenses, is a monthly worry.

Other agents talk about it. You do not know if it pays off at your income, or what it changes in your books.

"New furnace or new roof — can I write it off?"

Rent, mortgage interest, taxes and repairs for each property are mixed up. The T776 takes a weekend every spring.

Real Estate Bookkeeping FAQ

Do you do bookkeeping for realtors in Winnipeg?

Yes. We track commission income from your brokerage, your marketing, vehicle and phone costs, and file your GST. Our bookkeepers are in-house and located in Manitoba, near Winnipeg.

Do real estate agents have to charge GST on commissions?

Most active agents earn enough to have to register for GST and charge it on commissions. Once registered, you can usually claim back GST paid on business expenses. On the call, Jennifer checks where you stand.

How much of my commission should I set aside for tax?

It depends on your income, your expenses and whether you pay CRA instalments. We do not guess on a web page. On a free call, Jennifer gives you a set-aside approach that fits your numbers.

Should a real estate agent in Manitoba incorporate?

For some agents it helps, for others it only adds cost. It depends on your income and what you need to take out each year. On the call, we tell you whether incorporating makes sense for you. We make no promises about tax savings.

Is a rental property repair deductible, or is it a capital improvement?

A repair that restores something to its old condition is usually a current expense. An upgrade or a part that lasts for years is usually capital, and you claim it through CCA over time. CRA often reviews this line, so good records matter.

Can you do rental property bookkeeping for several properties?

Yes. We keep rent, mortgage interest, property tax, insurance and repairs separate for each property. At year-end you get clean numbers for your T776 rental statements.
