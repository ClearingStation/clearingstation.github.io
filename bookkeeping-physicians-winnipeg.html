Are you an accountant for doctors or a bookkeeper?

We are a Winnipeg bookkeeping and tax firm. Your dedicated bookkeeper keeps your books each month, and we prepare and file your corporate and personal tax returns.

Should I incorporate as a physician in Manitoba?

It depends on your income, your spending and your stage, for example a resident finishing training or a locum. There is no single answer. On the call, Jennifer tells you what to look at for your situation.

Should I pay myself salary or dividends from my MPC?

Each has trade-offs. Salary creates RRSP room and CPP contributions; dividends do not. The right mix depends on your income and plans, so we look at your numbers before we answer.

Do physicians charge GST?

Most medical services are exempt from GST. Some services, such as certain medical-legal reports or cosmetic work, can be taxable. We check what applies to your billings.

How do you protect my financial information?

Your data is protected with AES-256 encryption. All our bookkeepers are in-house and located in Manitoba. We handle your records under PIPEDA and PHIA.
