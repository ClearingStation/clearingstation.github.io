Common questions

E-Commerce Bookkeeping FAQ

Do you do Shopify bookkeeping?

Yes. We reconcile Shopify sales against Shopify Payments, PayPal and Stripe payouts, so fees, refunds and chargebacks are recorded, not lost. We do the same for WooCommerce stores.

Can you help Amazon and Etsy sellers in Canada?

Yes. Amazon and Etsy payouts include many fees and adjustments. We split each payout into sales, fees, refunds and tax, so your books show what really happened.

What sales tax do I charge customers in other provinces?

In general, GST/HST on goods shipped to another province follows the customer's province, not yours. Some provinces can also require you to register for their PST. The rules depend on your sales. Your bookkeeper files your GST/PST returns.

Do Etsy sellers have to pay taxes?

Yes. Income from an Etsy shop is business income and goes on your tax return, even as a side hustle. Good books let you claim your costs, like materials, shipping and fees.

Can you track inventory and cost of goods sold?

Yes. We record your stock purchases, freight and import duties as part of your cost of goods sold. That is how you see your real margin per product.

Do you only work with Winnipeg stores?

Our bookkeepers are in-house and located in Manitoba, near Winnipeg. We do online store bookkeeping for sellers across Manitoba.
