Tax filing in Winnipeg: common questions

What are the tax filing deadlines in Canada?

Personal tax returns are due April 30. If you or your spouse are self-employed, the filing deadline is June 15, but any balance owing is still due April 30. A corporate T2 return is due six months after the end of the corporation's fiscal year.

How much does tax preparation cost in Winnipeg?

Our tax preparation starts at $499 per return. You get a fixed quote on the free call. If you want bookkeeping and tax together, our Bookkeeping & Tax plan is $499/month and includes corporate T2 and owner and spouse personal returns.

Am I missing deductions as a self-employed person?

Many owners do. Home office costs, vehicle use, travel and everyday business expenses are often left off. We actively search for deductions that were not submitted. On the call we tell you which ones likely apply to you.

I missed the deadline. What happens now?

If you owe tax, the CRA charges a late-filing penalty and interest, and both grow the longer you wait. We file overdue returns, reduce penalties where possible, and handle the CRA communication for you. The sooner you file, the less it costs.

Can you fix or amend a return from a past year?

Yes. We file and amend overdue or incorrect returns. On the call we tell you if a review of last year's return is worth it. We do not promise a refund before we see the numbers.
