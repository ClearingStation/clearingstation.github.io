CRA deadlines

Which deadline is yours?

The date depends on what you file. These are the CRA rules for the returns we prepare.

What you fileFile byPay by
Personal return (T1)April 30April 30
Personal return, you or your spouse self-employedJune 15April 30
Corporate return (T2)Six months after your fiscal year endTwo months after your fiscal year end. Three months for some small Canadian-controlled private corporations.
T4 and T5 slipsLast day of FebruaryNothing to pay. Your employees get their T4 slips by the same day.
GST/HST, monthly or quarterlyOne month after the period endsSame day
GST/HST, annualThree months after your fiscal year endSame day
GST/HST, annual, sole proprietor with a December 31 year endJune 15April 30

If a date falls on a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday, the due date is the next business day. Source: Canada Revenue Agency, canada.ca. These are the general rules. On the call, Jennifer tells you your own date.

Tax filing in Winnipeg: common questions

What are the tax filing deadlines in Canada?

Personal tax returns are due April 30. If you or your spouse are self-employed, the filing deadline is June 15, but any balance owing is still due April 30. A corporate T2 return is due six months after the end of the corporation's fiscal year.

How much does tax preparation cost in Winnipeg?

A personal T1 return is $75 per person. A self-employed return with your T2125 is $450, or $900 if we also do your year of bookkeeping. A corporate T2 return is $450. If you want bookkeeping and tax together, our Bookkeeping & Tax plan is $499/month and includes corporate T2 and owner and spouse personal returns.

Am I missing deductions as a self-employed person?

Many owners do. Home office costs, vehicle use, travel and everyday business expenses are often left off. We actively search for deductions that were not submitted. On the call we tell you which ones likely apply to you.

I missed the deadline. What happens now?

If you owe tax, the CRA charges a late-filing penalty and interest, and both grow the longer you wait. We file overdue returns, reduce penalties where possible, and handle the CRA communication for you. The sooner you file, the less it costs.

Can you fix or amend a return from a past year?

Yes. We file and amend overdue or incorrect returns. On the call we tell you if a review of last year's return is worth it. We do not promise a refund before we see the numbers.

Do you file corporate T2 returns for small businesses?

Yes. We prepare corporate T2 returns with the required schedules, plus GST/PST filings. With tax-ready bookkeeping all year, year-end goes much smoother.
