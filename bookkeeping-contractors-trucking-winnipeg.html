Contractor and Trucking Bookkeeping FAQ

Do you do bookkeeping for contractors and construction companies in Manitoba?

Yes. We keep books for trades and construction businesses — plumbers, electricians, renovators and general contractors. That includes job costing, subcontractor payments, materials, equipment and GST/PST.

Do I need to file T5018 slips for my subcontractors?

Businesses whose main income is from construction usually must report payments to subcontractors on T5018 slips. Whether it applies to you depends on your business. Jennifer tells you on the call, and we can prepare the slips for you.

Do you do bookkeeping for truck drivers and owner-operators?

Yes. We track per-load income, fuel, maintenance, truck and trailer loans, and meal costs on the road. We also keep your records ready for fuel tax reporting if you cross provincial or U.S. borders.

Can you track which jobs or loads make money?

Yes. We set up your books so each job or load shows its income and its costs. Then you see which work pays and which work you should price higher or turn down.

How do you handle my truck, trailer and equipment?

We record loans and purchases correctly and track depreciation (capital cost allowance) for your year-end. Your accountant or our tax team gets clean numbers.
