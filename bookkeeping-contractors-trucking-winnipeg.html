Contractor Bookkeeping FAQ

What is a job costing report?

It shows each job on one line: the estimate, the costs so far (labour, materials, subs and equipment) and the margin. You see which jobs make money while they are still running.

What is a WIP schedule?

A work-in-progress schedule lists every open job with its contract value, percent complete, amount billed and amount earned. The difference shows if you are over-billed or under-billed on each job.

Why does my bank or bonding company ask for a WIP schedule?

It shows them how much work you have, how profitable it is and whether your billing is ahead of the work. Clean, monthly WIP schedules make credit and bonding conversations easier.

Do I need to file T5018 slips for my subcontractors?

Businesses whose main income is from construction usually must report payments to subcontractors on T5018 slips. Whether it applies to you depends on your business. Jennifer tells you on the call, and we can prepare the slips for you.

Do you work with truck drivers and owner-operators?

Yes. We track income per load, fuel, maintenance, and truck and trailer loans. We also keep your records ready for fuel tax reporting if you cross provincial or U.S. borders.
