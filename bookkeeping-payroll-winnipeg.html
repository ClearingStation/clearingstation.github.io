What we do, day by day

  1. 3 and 17 · Pay day We run the pay and deduct CPP, EI and income tax.
  2. 15 · CRA remittance due We send January's source deductions to the CRA. A regular remitter pays by the 15th of the next month.
  3. 28 · T4 deadline We file last year's T4 return. Your employees get their T4 slips by the last day of February.
  4. All month Your dedicated bookkeeper enters each pay run in your books.

Your remittance schedule can differ. The CRA sets it by the amount you withhold. If a due date falls on a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday, the due date is the next business day. Source: Canada Revenue Agency, canada.ca.
