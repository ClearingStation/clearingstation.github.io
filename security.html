How we protect your files

Four rules we never break.

Only you can log in

You log in with your password and a code on your phone, like your bank. Or use a YubiKey security key if you prefer. A stolen password alone opens nothing.

Every file is encrypted

Your files are encrypted when they are saved and when they are on the way to you.

Only our in-house team can see it

Every bookkeeper is our own employee in Manitoba. We do not send your books to freelancers or offshore teams.

Your books are always yours

Leave whenever you want. You get your full books and documents in standard formats like Excel and PDF, ready for your next bookkeeper or for QuickBooks. No fee, no lock-in.

Built for regulated work

Patient records, legal files and financial statements need more care than a shared folder. Our clients include physicians, clinics and law offices, and our systems are built for them.

The team behind our systems has years of experience building banking, messaging and accounting platforms. We use our own secure software, so your books do not pass through third-party apps. You never need to buy or learn bookkeeping software.
